OUR SCHOOL, OUR COMMUNITY

Welcome to Ceema Eagles Academy.

From Nursery to JHS, learners build knowledge, confidence and friendships in a caring school community.

Our motto, "Conquering to the top," reflects the steady progress we encourage in every learner.

With curiosity and kindness at the heart of school life, every learner is encouraged to take the next step with confidence.

OUR VISION

Confident learners, ready for what’s next.

We want every learner to grow in knowledge, character and confidence—from their first school experiences through JHS.

WHAT WE WORK TOWARD
  • Strong foundations at every learning stage
  • Curiosity, kindness and a sense of belonging
  • Steady progress toward each learner’s potential
