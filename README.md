School crest and photos were obtained from the publicly visible official Instagram profile https://www.instagram.com/ceemaeaglesacademy/. Gold, black and white are visually inferred from its crest; these are approximate web colors, not a supplied brand guide. Original posts are linked on the page. No contact details or application destinations were invented.
